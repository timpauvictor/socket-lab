## Findings retained as experimental limitations

### High: historical merge and evaluation are not equivalent

`initial/train_task.py:176-180` and `modal/train_task.py:188-194` use PEFT `combination_type="linear"`. The legacy evaluator also uses a linear factor merge at `initial/eval_translate.py:63`.

PEFT's [documented algorithm](https://huggingface.co/blog/peft_merging) combines the A and B factors separately. Their product contains cross terms. It is not generally the sum of the original adapter deltas. A scalar counterexample gives `(1+3)*(2+4)=24`, versus `1*2+3*4=14` for a delta sum. This is a demonstrated mismatch with the old documentation, not evidence of its numerical contribution to the recorded scores.

The historical merge setting is preserved, not silently replaced with a new experiment. The German-English task-plus-language row also names Romanian and English language adapters in `results/eval-board.csv:7`. Neither issue affects the monolithic adapter's no-language-adapter training design, but both constrain composition claims.

### Medium: resume and truncation need a real training validation before reuse

`initial/train_task.py:183` loads `args.output` for later pairs before Trainer checkpoint resume at line 221. A checkpoint alone is therefore not a demonstrated recovery path when the expected output adapter is absent. The exact installed PEFT/Trainer checkpoint interaction was not exercised; do not call this silent fresh training without evidence.

`initial/train_lora.py:141-206` explicitly implements a manual weight resume with fresh optimizer/scheduler and restarted data order. That is not an exact continuation. The current all-masked guard does not establish that all tokenizer boundary/truncation choices are optimal. No full training run or real-tokenizer corpus audit was performed.

### Medium: provenance and termination remain incomplete

Historical JSONL headers lack strategy, adapter hashes, finish reason, and generated token counts. The full rows do have matching IDs, source text, and references within each direction. New metadata improves declared configuration tracking but does not reconstruct missing historical facts or attest to server state.

The repetition metric requires at least 24 whitespace-separated words and three occurrences of an exact eight-word window, including overlapping windows. It cannot distinguish EOS termination from an output limit. The scratchpad run uses a different prompt and token/stop settings from direct and pivot requests.
